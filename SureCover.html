The NHIA Act 2022

REGULATORY CONTEXT

The Act makes health insurance mandatory for all Nigerians. This creates a flood of "First-Time Users" who are unsophisticated about policy limits.

  • Insight: New users are more likely to assume "Everything is free" and get angry when rejected.
  • SureCover Role: Acts as the educational layer for this new mass market.

Ilera Eko Case Study

MARKET PRECEDENT

Lagos State's scheme showed high enrollment via agents but faced challenges in utilization experience.

  • The Gap: Patients often travel to hospitals only to find their specific ailment isn't covered at that tier.
  • Lesson: Verification must happen before or at the point of care, not after billing.

Tech Realism in Hospitals

INFRASTRUCTURE

Most Lagos hospitals (especially General Hospitals) have dead zones for network coverage in Radiology/Wards.

  • Constraint: Apps that require heavy data or constant connection fail.
  • Solution: SureCover is a PWA (Progressive Web App). It caches the user's basic formulary data for offline checking.

How It Works

1

OCR Extraction

The app uses Tesseract.js (client-side) to read the doctor's handwritten or printed notes directly from the camera stream.

2

Fuzzy Logic Matching

Doctors often write brand names (e.g., "Augmentin"). Our algorithm maps this to the Generic Name ("Amoxicillin Clavulanate") listed in the Heirs formulary.

3

Real-Time Rule Check

It checks the user's specific plan limits (Gold/Silver/Bronze) instantly. If the brand isn't covered, it suggests the covered generic substitute.

Strengths (Internal)

  • Medical Logic: Designed by medical students who know the drugs.
  • Low Cost: PWA architecture is cheaper than native app maintenance.

Weaknesses (Internal)

  • Data Dependency: Requires clean API access to Heirs' formulary database.
  • OCR Accuracy: Doctors' handwriting is notoriously difficult (started with printed Rx focus).

Opportunities (External)

  • Inflation: Patients are price-sensitive and desperate to find covered drugs.
  • HR Partnerships: Corporate HR will push this app to reduce their own employee complaints.

Threats (External)

  • Hospital Resistance: Some providers profit from billing opacity and may discourage use.
